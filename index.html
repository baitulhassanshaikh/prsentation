The problem

A good small business can still be invisible, overloaded and unsafe online.

Meet Ayesha. She bakes amazing cakes, but nobody online knows her shop exists. To fix that she would need a designer, a web developer, an ad agency and a security expert: four people and four bills.

Invisible

No logo, website or ads, so customers never find her.

Too many tools

Design, website, ads and safety are separate, costly and confusing.

Unprotected

Phishing, fake payment links and hacked accounts. Around 4 in 10 cyberattacks hit small businesses (approximate).

~400 million

small businesses worldwide, around 5 million in Pakistan (both approximate).

Our solution

Four modules, one subscription. Every module shares one customer profile, so the ads match the brand and Shield protects the site we built.

Brand Studio

AI designer

Logo, colors, posters, social posts.

Ayesha gets a cake logo and menu poster in 1 hour.

Checked by student designers.

Site Builder

AI web developer

Website or store in the local language, built from a short chat.

A site with an “Order on WhatsApp” button, live in 3 hours.

Checked by a developer.

Growth Engine

AI advertiser + machine learning

Writes ads, picks audiences, learns what works.

It spots that Friday evening posts bring the most orders and moves the budget there.

Checked weekly by a marketer.

Shield

Cybersecurity + machine learning

Scans accounts, flags scam links, 2-step login, daily backups.

It flags a fake “courier payment” link and warns her in plain Urdu.

Checked by a security analyst.

  • Hour 0. She sends her shop name, photos and price list on WhatsApp.

Risks, rules and team

Risks and answers

  • AI mistakes: human review and a quality checklist.
  • Data leak: encryption, least-access rules, regular audits.
  • Trust: free first month and results shown openly.
  • Copycats: local language, human support, data that improves with every customer.
  • AI costs: cheaper models for simple tasks.

Our honest rules

  • No fake reviews, followers or misleading ads.
  • Customers own their data; we never sell it.
  • A human checks AI work before it is used.
  • We say plainly what is automated and what a person did.
  • We follow local privacy and advertising laws.

Team

  • Founder: vision, customers, partners.
  • Tech and security leads: hired later.
  • Students and freelancers: reviewers paid per task.
  • Support: WhatsApp, in the owner’s language.

Students under 18 do low-risk tasks only, never handle money or private data, and work with a parent’s permission.
